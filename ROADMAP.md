## Changelog

Notable player-facing changes.

### 2026-08

- **Gate progression now requires three or more distinct paying
  wallets.** Closes off unlocking tournament and VIP gates by playing
  against yourself. Pots are unaffected and always pay out in full.
  ([why](docs/RULES.md#gate-progression))
- **Paid rooms lock once a match starts.** Nobody joins a fight already
  in progress; late arrivals get a fresh room instead.
- **Buy-ins are refunded if you leave before a match starts.**
  Previously a stake in a match that had not begun could be left behind.
- **Wallet sign-in for paid entry.** Entering a paid gate now requires a
  signature from your wallet, so nobody can enter a paid match using
  someone else's address.
- Fixed a bug where walking through a gate could bounce you back to the
  title screen.
