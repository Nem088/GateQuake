# FAQ

**Do I need a wallet to play?**
No. The Practice gate is free and needs no wallet, no deposit and no
signature. You only need one for paid gates.

**Do I need to download anything?**
No. It runs in the browser.

**Which wallets work?**
Phantom is what we test against. Other Solana wallets that support
message signing should work.

**What is GQ?**
The in-game credit. 1 USDC = 1,000 GQ when you deposit; 1,000 GQ = 0.97
USDC when you withdraw. It is not a token, it is not tradeable, and
there was never a presale. See
[Fair play & security](SECURITY.md#your-money).

**What does GateQuake charge?**
The 3% spread between depositing and withdrawing. Nothing else — no
rake on pots, no fee on top of a buy-in, no subscription.

**What are the minimums?**
$5 to deposit, $1 to withdraw.

**Can I withdraw to an exchange?**
Yes — any Solana address you control, including an exchange USDC
deposit address. Make sure it accepts **USDC on Solana**. Wrong network
means gone.

**How long do deposits take?**
Usually under a minute once the transfer confirms on Solana. You get a
notification in-game when it lands.

**What happens to my buy-in if I leave?**
Before the match starts, it is refunded automatically. After it starts,
it stays in the pot. See [Match rules](RULES.md#your-buy-in).

**What if the server dies mid-match?**
Every buy-in is refunded automatically. We do not keep money from a
match that failed.

**I won a 1v1 but my win count didn't go up. Why?**
Progression toward tournament and VIP gates only counts matches with at
least three different paying wallets, which stops people farming those
unlocks by playing against themselves. Your pot was paid in full — only
the stat is held back. See
[Match rules](RULES.md#gate-progression).

**Is there a token / NFT / airdrop coming?**
No. Anyone telling you otherwise is scamming you using our name.

**Can I play on my phone?**
It runs in a mobile browser, but it is built for mouse and keyboard and
you will be at a real disadvantage. Practice is free — try before you
stake anything.
