# How to play

GateQuake runs in your browser. There is nothing to download.

## 1. Pick a name and enter

Go to [gatequake.com](https://gatequake.com), type a name, and step
through. You land in the **lobby** — a walled plaza with a row of
archways along the far side. Those are the gates.

## 2. Try Practice first

The **PRACTICE** gate is free and needs no wallet. It drops you into a
real arena with bots and creatures. Every mechanic works exactly as it
does in a paid match: the quakes still come, the floor still falls away,
you still die. You just respawn instead of losing anything.

Spend a few rounds here. The arena punishes people who have not learned
where the ground goes.

## 3. Walk through a gate

Gates open on contact — walk into one and you are in. Each archway is
labelled with its buy-in and what the winner takes from a full room.

Paid gates need a connected wallet and enough GQ. See
[Match rules](RULES.md) for what happens to your buy-in.

## 4. Survive

Between 2 and 36 fighters spawn on pedestals around a central obelisk.
Once the match starts the quake cycle begins:

| Phase | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Calm** | Nothing falls. Fight, or reposition. |
| **Tremor** | Doomed tiles flash red. Get off them. |
| **Quake** | Those tiles drop into the void. So does anything standing on them. |

Each quake eats another ring of the perimeter and punches holes through
the interior. The arena only ever gets smaller.

Last one alive wins the pot.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `W` `A` `S` `D` | Move |
| Mouse | Look |
| `Shift` | Sprint |
| `C` | Crouch |
| `Space` | Jump — press again in the air to double-jump |
| `X` | Glide (hold while falling) |
| Left click | Primary attack |
| Right click | Alternate attack — throws knife or grenade |
| `Q` + click | Modifier attack |
| `E` + click | Power attack |
| `Alt` | Parry — briefly reduces incoming damage |
| `1` – `4` | Fists / Knife / Sword / Hammer |
| `E` | Open the Store at the kiosk |
| `R` (hold) | Glance behind you |
| `0` | First-person / third-person |
| `Esc` | Pause, wallet, settings |

## Weapons

Each weapon has four attacks depending on the button and modifier.

- **Fists** — fast, weak, always available.
- **Knife** — quick stabs, and the only weapon you can *throw*.
- **Sword** — good reach, and a spinning slash that hits all around you.
- **Hammer** — slowest and heaviest. Its power attack smashes the tile
  in front of you straight into the void, which is as useful for making
  escape routes as it is for dropping someone else.

## Pickups

They spawn through the match and are contested.

- **Health pod** — restores health.
- **Grenade pod** — a live grenade with a short fuse. Right click throws
  it. It voids tiles where it lands.
- **Jetpack pod** — a few seconds of flight.
- **Shield crystal** — several seconds of complete immunity to damage.
  It will **not** save you from falling into the void.
