## What we collect

**Your wallet address.** Your public Solana address is your identity on
the Service. We never receive, request or store your seed phrase or
private keys.

**Gameplay data.** Match participation, results, buy-ins, payouts,
balances and lifetime statistics. We keep this because it is the record
of your money and the evidence in any dispute.

**Transaction records.** Deposits and withdrawals, including on-chain
transaction identifiers and destination addresses you provide.

**Technical data.** IP address, approximate location derived from it,
browser and device characteristics, connection timings and error logs.
We use this to run the Service, enforce regional restrictions, and
detect abuse.

**Anything you send us.** Emails, support requests and reports.

**A chosen display name.** Whatever you type. Please do not use your
real name.

## What we do not collect

- No seed phrase or private key, ever.
- No real name, address, date of birth or identity document, unless we
  are legally required to verify identity — in which case we will tell
  you before asking.
- No third-party advertising or tracking cookies.
- No payment card details. We never touch them.

## Cookies and local storage

We use browser local storage for things the game needs to work: your
display name, cosmetic choices, and a session token that proves your
browser is authorised for paid entry. These are functional, not
advertising, and are not shared.

## Why we are allowed to use it

Depending on where you live, our legal bases include: **performance of
a contract** (running the game and paying you), **legal obligation**
(anti-money-laundering, sanctions, tax and regulatory record-keeping),
and **legitimate interests** (security, fraud and cheat prevention,
service improvement).

## Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with:

- **Infrastructure and hosting providers** that operate the Service on
  our behalf.
- **Blockchain networks.** Any on-chain transaction is public and
  permanent by design. We cannot make a blockchain forget.
- **Regulators, law enforcement or courts** where legally required.
- **A successor entity** if the Service is sold or reorganised.

## The public-ledger caveat

Blockchain transactions are public. Anyone can inspect deposits and
withdrawals associated with an address. **This is a property of Solana,
not a choice we made, and we cannot undo it.** If you need financial
privacy, understand this before you deposit.

## Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access,
correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your data, to data
portability, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection
authority.

Write to `[PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL]` and we will respond within the period
the law requires.

**One honest limit:** we may be unable to delete records we are legally
required to keep, and we can never delete anything already written to a
blockchain.

from children. If we discover an underage account we will close it.
