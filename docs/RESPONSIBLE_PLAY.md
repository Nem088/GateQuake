# Responsible play

**GateQuake is played for real money. You can lose it.**

Read this page even if you skip the others.

## The basics

- You must be **18 or older** (or the legal age where you are, if it is
  higher).
- It must be **legal where you live**. Real-money competitive gaming is
  restricted or prohibited in many places. Checking is your
  responsibility — see [Terms & Conditions](TERMS.md#eligibility).
- Play only with money you can afford to lose entirely.

## What losing looks like here

Every paid match takes your buy-in up front. Most matches, you do not
win it back — in a full 36-player room, 35 people go home with nothing.
That is not a flaw in the game, it is the shape of the game.

Skill genuinely matters. It does not make you safe. Better players lose
matches constantly, and a losing streak can be long and expensive.

The gates are designed to escalate. Moving up a tier multiplies your
stake by ten. That is fun when it goes well and ruinous when it does
not, and the game will happily let you do it.

## Warning signs

Stop and take stock if any of this sounds like you:

- Playing a higher tier than you meant to, to win back a loss.
- Playing with money set aside for something else.
- Hiding how much you play, or how much you have lost.
- Depositing again immediately after telling yourself you were done.
- Feeling like you are owed a win because of a bad run. You are not —
  every match is independent of the last.
- Playing to escape stress, boredom, or low mood rather than for fun.

Chasing losses is the single most reliable way to turn a small loss into
a large one.

## Practical limits

- **Decide your budget before you connect a wallet, not during a losing
  run.** Deposit that amount and nothing more.
- **Withdraw winnings instead of rolling them.** A balance you can see
  in the game does not feel like money. It is money.
- **Keep the bulk of your funds in your own wallet.** Only what you are
  actively playing with needs to be a GQ balance.
- **Set a time limit as well as a money limit.** Long sessions make bad
  decisions.
- **Never play impaired, tilted, or half-asleep.**

## Taking a break

Want to lock yourself out for a while, or permanently? Contact us at
**support@gatequake.com** and say so. We will close your access. Tell us how
long, and we will hold you to it.

You do not need to justify the request and we will not try to talk you
out of it.

## Getting help

If gambling is affecting your life, money, work, or relationships, talk
to someone independent of us. Free, confidential help exists in most
countries:

- **International directory:** [Gambling Therapy](https://www.gamblingtherapy.org) — free online support, many languages
- **United Kingdom:** [BeGambleAware](https://www.begambleaware.org) — 0808 8020 133
- **United States:** [National Problem Gambling Helpline](https://www.ncpgambling.org/help-treatment/) — call or text 1-800-522-4700
- **Canada:** [Problem Gambling Institute of Ontario](https://www.problemgambling.ca)
- **Australia:** [Gambling Help Online](https://www.gamblinghelponline.org.au) — 1800 858 858

We are not a treatment service and we have an obvious conflict of
interest in advising you. These people do not.
