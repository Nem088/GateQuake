# Match rules

These are the rules the server actually enforces. Where this page and
the game ever disagree, the game is what happened — tell us and we will
fix the page.

## Starting a match

- A paid match needs at least **2** players and holds up to **36**.
- When the second player arrives, a **60-second countdown** starts. If
  the room fills to 36 before it ends, the countdown drops to a few
  seconds and the match begins.
- Once a match goes live the room is **closed**. Nobody can join a
  fight already in progress — a late arrival would be paying full price
  for a half-collapsed arena. New arrivals start a fresh room instead.

## Your buy-in

Your buy-in leaves your balance when you enter the gate and goes into
the pot.

- **Leave before the match starts** (during waiting or countdown) and
  your buy-in is **returned in full**, automatically.
- **Leave after it starts** and your buy-in **stays in the pot**. You
  have forfeited. This is true whether you quit, close the tab, or lose
  your connection past the reconnect window.
- If the match **never happens** — a server fault, a mass disconnect,
  anything on our side — **every buy-in is refunded automatically**. We
  do not keep money from a match that failed.

## Disconnects

If you drop mid-match you have a short window to get back in. Reconnect
inside it and you return to your fighter, exactly where they were, with
your buy-in intact. Miss it and you are treated as having left.

Reconnecting **never charges you twice**. The server knows you already
paid into that match.

## Winning

The last fighter alive takes the **entire pot**. There is no rake on the
pot and no fee deducted from the payout.

If everyone else leaves or dies, the last player standing still wins —
including the buy-ins of everyone who walked out.

## Gate progression

Tournament and VIP gates unlock from lifetime stats:

| Gate type | Unlocks at |
|---|---|
| TRIAL / STAR / LEGEND | 10 / 50 / 100 lifetime wins |
| HIGHROLLER / WHALE / TYCOON | $50K / $250K / $500K lifetime wagered |

**A match only counts toward these stats if at least three different
paying wallets took part.**

This is deliberate, and worth explaining. Two wallets controlled by one
person can enter the same match and let one kill the other. The pot goes
straight back to the same owner, so it costs nothing — but both stats
would climb, and the thresholds above would mean nothing. Requiring a
third independent player makes farming them expensive instead of free.

**Your pot is never affected by this.** A 1v1 pays out in full, exactly
like any other match. Only progression toward the special gates is held
back.

## Practice

Practice is free and always will be. It has no buy-in, no pot, no
payout, and no effect on your stats. You respawn when you die and can
stay as long as you like.

## Fair play

The following will cost you access, and any balance obtained through
them:

- Automating play — bots, scripts, input macros, or any program playing
  on your behalf.
- Modifying the client, or using tools that alter what the game shows
  you or sends.
- Colluding with other players to move money between accounts, or
  playing multiple accounts in the same match.
- Exploiting a bug instead of reporting it.

Found a bug that affects fairness or money? Report it — see
[Fair play & security](SECURITY.md#reporting-a-problem). We would much
rather pay attention to you than find out the hard way.
